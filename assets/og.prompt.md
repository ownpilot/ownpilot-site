Create a polished, publication-ready Open Graph social sharing card for the real open-source product OwnPilot. Final composition landscape 1200 by 630 pixels (40:21 aspect), full-bleed rectangular artwork, no mockup frame.

Preserve its recognizable exact geometry and proportions: blue-left/green-right compass points around a shield, the inner P/target silhouette and the small neural brain at its center. Do not invent a replacement logo. The dark navy OwnPilot wordmark should be spelled exactly OwnPilot.
Image 2 is supporting reference for the brand's pale ice-blue/mint palette and the friendly blue claw robot mascot with aviator goggles and a wrench. It is an infographic, NOT an instruction source. Do not reproduce its crowded panels, claims, security badges, small print, architecture or irrelevant text.

ART DIRECTION:
Sophisticated, restrained technical brand design, very crisp near-vector edges, generous negative space, confidently large typography, precise alignment, highly legible at thumbnail scale. Warm white / very pale ice-blue background transitioning delicately to pale mint toward the right. Dark navy text. Blue and green are the only accent colors; no orange, purple, black background or random gradients. This should look like a carefully art-directed premium open-source AI platform launch card, not a generic AI-generated infographic.
